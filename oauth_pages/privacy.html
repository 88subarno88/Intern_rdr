Privacy policy

Internship Radar is a personal, single-user automation. The only Google account it accesses is its owner's.

What it accesses

With the owner's permission it reads messages in the owner's Gmail inbox that match a search for internship and job-alert mails, and it sends digest mails from that same account to that same account.

How the data is used

Matching messages are processed on the owner's own computer to pick out relevant opportunities. Short excerpts are sent to an AI model (Anthropic's Claude) to be rated. Nothing is sold, shared with anyone else, or used for advertising.

Storage

Working files are kept on the owner's computer only and can be deleted at any time. Access can be withdrawn at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact

Questions go to the owner of this page through the GitHub account that hosts it.
